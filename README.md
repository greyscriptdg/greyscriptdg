## About

I'm a versatile technology professional who transitioned from an Electronics & Communication background into IT, with a self-taught journey into app development. Leadership didn't replace the developer in me, it built on top of it. I still stay close to the code because that's what makes the technical calls credible. My expertise spans frontend development (React), Agile project management, and technical leadership, with a focus on delivering scalable, high-quality applications.

As VP of Technology at GreyScript Technologies, I split my time between staying hands-on in development and leading from the front: overseeing software development, Agile execution, and technical strategy, while ensuring seamless collaboration and efficient product delivery. As a Certified Scrum Master (CSM), I apply Scrum methodologies to streamline workflows, improve team productivity, and help teams build impactful digital solutions.
